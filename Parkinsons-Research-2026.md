# Parkinson's Disease Research — October 2026

*Saved October 5, 2026. Deep research summary from conversation with Grok.*

---

## The Big News: Tavapadon Approved

**September 28, 2026** — FDA approved tavapadon (marketed as Juvmo), a first-in-class D1/D5 dopamine partial agonist. Once-daily oral pill for early Parkinson's.

- TEMPO-2 phase 3 trial (304 patients, 75 sites, 13 countries): 9.1-point improvement in MDS-UPDRS Parts II and III (motor symptoms + daily function) vs placebo at week 26.
- First new early-monotherapy approval beyond levodopa in decades.
- Key safety warnings apply — talk to a neurologist.

## Stem Cell Therapy: Sasineprocel (Aspen Neuroscience)

- FDA granted **RMAT designation** (Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy) — fast-track pathway, potential priority review.
- ASPIRO trial: 8 patients transplanted with autologous dopamine progenitor cells (made from their own skin fibroblasts — avoids chronic immunosuppression).
- 12-month results: no serious surgical adverse events, no graft-induced dyskinesia. Good ON time increased 2.1–2.4 hours. Six of seven reduced dopaminergic medication.
- Phase 3 targeted for later 2026.

## Environmental Causes Getting Sharper

- **Chlorpyrifos**: long-term residential exposure linked to 2.5x increased Parkinson's risk (UCLA, Molecular Neurodegeneration).
- **Paraquat**: now considered basically proven to cause Parkinson's — used in lab models because it's so reliably toxic to dopamine neurons.
- **Gene-environment interaction**: certain genetic variants are harmless alone but fail under pesticide stress, causing toxic buildup in brain cells (lysosomal/autophagy disruption).
- UCLA leading a $9M three-year study on pesticides and air pollution pathways.
- Paraquat Prevention Act introduced in US House, June 2026.

## What Failed in 2026

- **BIIB122** (Biogen/Denali, LRRK2 inhibitor): LUMA phase 2b failed primary endpoint — did not slow progression. Discontinued for idiopathic Parkinson's.
- **BIA 28-6156** (GCase modulator): negative phase 2, discontinued.
- Reminder: most candidates don't succeed. No disease-modifying therapy is approved yet.

## Other Pipeline Items

- **Prasinezumab** (anti-alpha-synuclein antibody): Phase 3 PARAISO, primary completion June 2029.
- **AB-1005** (GDNF gene therapy): REGENERATE-PD phase 2, completion ~2028.
- **Solengepras** (GPR6 inhibitor): ARISE phase 3 data expected end of Q3 2026.
- **Psilocybin** (UCSF pilot): 12 patients, tolerated without serious side effects; clinically significant improvements in mood, cognition, and motor function lasting weeks. First psychedelic tested in any neurodegenerative disease.

## Genetics

- ~13% of people have a Parkinson's-related genetic variant (PD GENEration study, up from earlier 5–10% estimates).
- GBA1: most common genetic risk factor, 5–30x increased risk.
- LRRK2: most common familial gene, up to 40% lifetime penetrance in Ashkenazi Jewish and North African Arab Berber populations.

## Risk Factors (UK Biobank, 311,000+ participants)

- Single risk factor: HR 1.43. Additive pairs: HR 2.00. Additive triplets: HR 4.21.
- Highest-risk triplets included low handgrip strength, epilepsy, hearing loss, diabetes.
- Loneliness appeared in top-risk pairs.

## Standard Treatment (unchanged)

- Levodopa (carbidopa-levodopa) remains the gold standard for motor symptoms.
- Deep brain stimulation helps motor symptoms but does not slow progression.
- No cure. No disease-modifying therapy approved yet.
