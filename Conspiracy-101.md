- Russia needs Iran's drones for Ukraine.
- Iran needs Russia's satellite intelligence and upgraded drones to strike US targets in the Gulf.
- The US is fighting both at once, and its own oil producers profit from the elevated prices.

The profiteers are the middlemen, not the fighters.

### Trump's Inner Circle

The clearest case. The Lukoil oil deal is tied to Todd Boehly, a two-million-dollar Trump donor, and two Middle Eastern groups that did business with Kushner or Witkoff's families. Witkoff co-founded World Liberty Financial, and the deal keeps surfacing in the Russia talks.

### The Gulf States

The quiet winners. Saudi Arabia, Iraq, and the UAE sell oil at a hundred-plus dollars a barrel while their biggest rival gets strangled, and they don't fire a shot.

### Russia

Profits from the drone loop: Iran's Shaheds fill the gap Ukraine's strikes created in Russian missile production, and Russia sells the upgraded versions back to Iran. Both sides are broke, so they trade technology instead of cash.

### The Losers

The people actually fighting — Iranian civilians, Ukrainian hospital patients, and American consumers paying a hundred-dollar oil bill. The war's a wealth transfer from the bottom to the middlemen.
